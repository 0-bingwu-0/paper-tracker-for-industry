# Weekly Summary

Digests make sure nothing slips by; the weekly summary works out what it means: what this week's papers add up to, and which of my views they support or challenge. It doesn't rescan papers; it builds on the week's digests.

## Prepare

* Read every digest in [output/digests/](../output/digests/) since the last weekly summary. With no weekly summary yet, read the last 7 days of digests.
* If some days were never scanned, run [What's New](scan.md) for them first.
* Upvotes have mostly settled by now. Take another look with `node workflows/scripts/hf-papers.mjs list 7`: papers the digests skipped whose upvotes climbed high may be what the field is really paying attention to, and are worth a read.

## Summary

Save it to `output/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, then tell me the week's main conclusion in a few sentences and link the summary.

* **Open with the one or two things that matter most this week.** Not a list of papers, but what they add up to.
* **Go view by view.** Which views were supported and which were challenged, a few sentences each, citing the papers. Skip views with no new evidence.
* **Directions outside the watchlist.** If one kept showing up this week, say so, and suggest whether to add it to the watchlist.
* **Same style as the digest:** conclusion first, framed in what it changes for my work, sources on every point, limits stated plainly.

## Update research

After the summary, add the week's new evidence to the matching research files in [output/research/](../output/research/), following the saving rules in [research.md](research.md). In your reply, list which research files you updated and what changed in each. If a view's conclusion changed, put the suggested new wording in the summary; update the watchlist only after I agree.
