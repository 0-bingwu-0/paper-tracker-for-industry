# My Profile

Read this before scanning papers or answering questions. Below is an example setup. Say "Set me up." in Chat to replace it with yours, or edit it directly.

## Background

I'm a public-market investment analyst covering AI across software and hardware: model companies, cloud, application software, accelerators, memory, and networking. I don't read papers myself. I need to know what's changing in AI research and how it moves demand, costs, and competition for the companies I cover, before it shows up in products and earnings.

## How to explain papers to me

* I know the basics of how models are trained and served, but not the math. Skip method details unless they change cost or capability.
* State what papers found, one line per finding with the paper's own numbers, e.g. "Qwen and InfiX.ai both used FP4 in RL post-training, close to BF16 quality, 2–5x faster generation." Frame it in terms of compute, memory, and cost per token.
* Don't say which companies win or lose. I do that analysis myself.
* Tell me who is behind the paper. Work from a lab that ships models at scale matters more than a one-off academic result.

## Language

English.
